Privacy Policy

Shape of Then is a personal tool used by one person to manage their own YouTube channel, "The Shape of Then". It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses. With the channel owner's permission, it reads the channel's videos and statistics and updates titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails of the owner's own videos through the YouTube API.

What it stores. Statistics and change logs are stored on the owner's own computer and in a public repository (view counts and text changes only). No personal data of viewers is collected. Sign-in tokens stay on the owner's computer.

Sharing. Data is not sold or shared with third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
